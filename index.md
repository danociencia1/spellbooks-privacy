# Privacy Policy for Spellbooks: Idle RPG

**Last updated: October 5, 2026**

This policy explains how **danogames**, the developer of **Spellbooks: Idle RPG**, handles information related to the game.

Contact: **danogames1@hotmail.com**

## 1. How the game works

Spellbooks: Idle RPG works offline and does not require an account or sign-in.

The current version does not include advertisements, in-app purchases, analytics services, or advertising trackers.

## 2. Data stored on your device

The game stores information locally to save your progress and preferences, including:

- Details of the character you create in the game.
- Your progress, inventory, equipment, spells, and earned resources.
- Gameplay statistics and the time of your last session, used to calculate offline progress.
- Sound preferences and other game settings.

This information is used to operate the game. The application does not send it to danogames servers or share it with third parties.

You do not need to provide real personal information to play.

## 3. Personal information and permissions

The game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or personal files.

It may use your device’s vibration feature to provide gameplay feedback. This feature does not collect personal information.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

Your saved game and settings remain in the application’s local storage until deleted.

You can delete your progress using the option to delete your saved game in the game’s settings. This removes your saved game but may retain your preferences.

To remove all locally stored application data, clear the application’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. Deleting your saved game will result in the loss of your progress.

danogames does not keep a copy of your saved game on its servers.

## 5. Email support

If you contact **danogames1@hotmail.com**, we receive your email address and any information you voluntarily include in your message.

We use this information to respond to your enquiry and provide support. Emails are processed through our email provider, Microsoft Outlook/Hotmail, and retained for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

You may request deletion of your messages by contacting the same address, unless we are legally required to retain them. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

## 6. External services

This policy describes information handling by the game and danogames.

Google Play and the Android operating system may process information under their own privacy policies. Such processing is separate from the game’s local storage of your progress.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the game’s information handling practices change.

If a future version introduces advertisements or other services that process data, we will update this policy before distributing that version and provide any required notices and consent mechanisms.
