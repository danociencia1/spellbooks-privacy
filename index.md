Privacy Policy – Spellbooks: Idle RPG
Developer: danogames · Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play Spellbooks: Idle RPG ("the game") on Android, and what choices you have. By using the game you agree to this policy.

1. Summary
The game does not require an account, sign-in or any real personal information.
Your game progress is stored only on your device.
The game shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one (for example, to double your offline gold or to open the bat's chest in the tavern). The game never shows ads you did not ask for.
There are no in-app purchases.
The developer does not run any server and does not collect or sell your data.
2. Data stored on your device
To save your game, the following information is stored locally on your device: your character, progress, inventory, equipment, spells, resources, session timing (used for offline rewards and timers) and settings such as sound and vibration. This data is not sent to the developer or shared with anyone by the developer.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show optional rewarded video ads. When the ads service is used, Google may automatically collect and process certain information from your device, such as:

The device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location (country or city);
Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language);
Interactions with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or watched) and diagnostic or performance data.
Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, show personalized ads. This processing is carried out by Google, not by the developer. The developer only receives aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown) and cannot identify you from them.

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)
If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message (provided by Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time ads are needed. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads and the related use of data. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings → Ad privacy ("Privacidad de los anuncios") option.

5. Your choices
Don't watch ads: ads are always optional; you can play the whole game without watching any.
Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
Delete your game data: you can delete your save from the game's settings. To remove all app data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. The developer keeps no copy of your save.
6. Permissions
The game uses internet access only to load and show ads. It does not access your contacts, location (GPS), camera, microphone or personal files. It may use vibration for gameplay feedback, which does not collect personal data.

7. Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us using the email below.

8. Email support
If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the content of your message are processed through Microsoft Outlook/Hotmail and kept only as long as needed to answer you. You can ask for your messages to be deleted. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

9. Third-party services
Google Play and Android may process data according to their own privacy policies. Google AdMob processes data as described in section 3.

10. Changes to this policy
If this policy changes (for example, if new services are added), the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date, before the version of the game that requires it is released.
